Tạo storewide discount cho store

Storewide discount (giảm giá toàn cửa hàng) nghĩa là khi bạn bật discount, **tất cả sản phẩm/campaign đang có trong store** sẽ được áp dụng discount đó (khách hàng sẽ thấy giá giảm khi vào giỏ/checkout).

Ví dụ: nếu bạn tạo discount **10%**, sản phẩm có giá **$36.99** khi vào giỏ sẽ còn **$33.29** (làm tròn 2 chữ số thập phân).

Khi discount đang hoạt động, storefront sẽ hiển thị banner trên cùng để khách hàng biết, ví dụ: xuất hiện ở đầu trang home (http://localhost:5173/) cùng hàng logo + about|.....

"10% off all orders applied in cart"

Step 1: Navigate to the Discount Screen

Click on 'Discounts' in the left-hand navigation menu of your Mayzing account to reach the discount screen.

‍

Step 2: Create a New Discount

Click on the 'Create a New Discount' button on the discount screen. A pop-up window will appear, prompting you to select whether your discount will be storewide or campaign-specific. Choose 'Storewide'.

‍

Step 3: Select Discount Type

Decide whether the discount will offer free shipping or a percentage off all purchases. Select the appropriate option.

‍

Step 4: Choose the Audience

Next, you have to select the audience for your discount. 'Everyone' is suitable for an untargeted discount, which will apply to all customers. For a targeted discount that only applies to customers with a specific link or promo code, choose 'Targeted'.

‍

Step 5: Create the Discount

Click 'Create Discount'. You will now be redirected to the discount details page.

‍

Step 6: Define Discount Details

On the discount details page, you can:

‍

Assign a name to your discount.

If you've chosen a percentage discount, set the percentage off.

For targeted discounts, define a unique promo code.

Specify a minimum cart value to qualify for the discount, if desired.

After filling in these details, click 'Create Discount'.


Step 7: Activate the Discount

Back on the discount screen, you can toggle your new discount on and off. To make it live in your store, click 'Publish Changes'. Your storewide discount is now active and ready to use.
