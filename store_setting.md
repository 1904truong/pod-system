From the left-hand navigation menu, click on 'Store Settings'. 

The click on branding.

In the logo section, drag your new image in, or press browse, find the logo file, and press upload.  The recommended file format is JPEG or PNG with dimensions of at least 800 x 500 px.  

In the banner section, drag your new image in, or press browse, find the banner file, and press upload.  We recommend using a JPEG or PNG file with dimensions of at least 1100 x 300 px.

In the favicon section drag your new image in or press browse and upload.  Your file must be 32 x 32 pixels and in an ICO, PNG, GIF, or JPG format

The 'About Us' page is an opportunity to share your story, build customer trust, and convey what sets your business apart. You can include details about what you sell and what makes you unique.

In this section, add your desired text.  (Please note there is a limit of 1000 characters) 

Once you've entered your text, click Save Description

Now click on layouts and features.

Under desktop layout you can choose to have 3 columns or 4 columns, press save settings if you make changes here

For mobile layout you can choose to have 1 or 2 columns, press save settings if you make changes here.

You can choose to hide the filters in your store by toggling the option off and pressing save settings

You can also remove the search function from your store by toggling off the search option, and pressing save settings.

You can choose whether you want the first 12 products in your store to load first, or 24.  12 gives you the optimum speed of loading, but you may want to show 24.  Press save settings to complete the change.
